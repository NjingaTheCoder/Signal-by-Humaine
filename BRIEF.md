# Humaine — company brief (source of truth for product/copy decisions)

Researched live from wearehumaine.com on 2026-07-10. This exists so future work on Signal (or any
other Humaine product) starts from Humaine's actual positioning instead of a generic "marketing
agency" assumption.

## Positioning

- **Tagline**: "Human Judgement, AI Speed"
- **Category claim**: "The AI Digital Marketing Agency Built Around Commercial Outcomes"
- **Core thesis**: "human judgement, accelerated by AI, produces better commercial outcomes"
- **What they sell**: "Strategy, creative production, AI-native workflows and search visibility
  into one connected growth system"
- **Mission framing**: "We help businesses and people thrive in the age of AI"
- Site-wide tagline used elsewhere: "Extraordinary Together"

## How Humaine differentiates itself from traditional agencies

| Dimension | Traditional agency | Humaine |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Campaign brief | Commercial problem |
| AI's role | Bolt-on tool | Core workflow layer |
| Human's role | Executes the brief | Applies judgement at key decision points |
| Output | Campaigns | Connected growth system |
| Handover | Agency dependency | Transferable capability |

Explicit contrast with other AI agencies: "Most AI Agencies Start With the Technology. We Start
With the People." — i.e. Humaine's pitch is not "we have AI tools," it's "we apply human judgement
faster because of AI."

## The Six Labs (service structure)

1. **Strategy Lab** — "AI can generate answers quickly. The harder part is knowing which questions
   matter." Brand/marketing strategy, positioning, messaging, channel planning, AI readiness
   assessment, commercial roadmaps.
2. **Content Lab** — "The challenge isn't producing more content, it's producing content that
   matters." Content strategy, editorial planning, campaigns, SEO-ready copy, scalable content
   systems.
3. **Production Lab** — Balances "high-end creative production with AI-assisted versioning." Film,
   photography, editing, campaign assets, social content.
4. **Search Lab** — "AI assistants, marketplaces and discovery platforms increasingly shape how
   people find and choose brands." SEO, **AEO** (answer engine optimization), content
   restructuring, schema markup, marketplace optimization.
5. **Tech + IP Lab** — "The infrastructure that connects data, platforms, workflows and teams into
   one usable system." AI systems, automation, integrations, dashboards, proprietary tools.
6. **Brand Lab** — brand identity/quality work, integrated with Strategy Lab in practice.

## Who they work with

- **B2B**: B2B technology and SaaS businesses navigating AI transformation "at a pace your
  organisation can absorb." Client example: Sage, Six Degrees.
- **Retail**: Retail/fashion brands modernising without losing brand quality or team capacity.
  Client examples: ASICS, Wax London, Debenhams.
- Both segments described as "marketing teams under pressure" needing "strategy, production, and
  distribution to work as one system."

## Tone of voice

Professional, direct, partnership-oriented ("no egos, no nonsense"). Leads with commercial outcomes
and judgement, not tooling or jargon. Avoids hype language ("AI will replace...") in favour of
augmentation framing (human judgement + AI speed).

## Implications for Signal (this app)

- Signal's news categories should track what Humaine's Six Labs and client base actually care
  about: AI-native marketing workflows (not just "martech" broadly), AEO/answer-engine visibility
  as a distinct concern from classic SEO, B2B SaaS GTM, and retail brand modernisation — not just
  "marketing industry news" in general.
- Copy should echo the augmentation framing (human judgement, AI speed) rather than either
  AI-hype language or a generic industry-newsletter voice.
- Humaine is Cape Town–based with a global B2B/retail client base — no reason to weight sources
  toward any single region beyond what's already in the brief (UK/EU tenders, US/UK news, ZA via
  Bizcommunity).
